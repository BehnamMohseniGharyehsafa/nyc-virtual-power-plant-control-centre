# Implemented scope and outstanding work

The application is a functioning reduced-order research simulator. The master brief is broader than the validated implementation. The following limits must remain visible in publications and demonstrations.

* **Empirical calibration:** LL84 matching/calibration, measured building interval demand, roof obstructions/shading surveys, verified HVAC types and occupancy calibration are not implemented. Floor counts/areas are derived estimates. PLUTO provides lot-level information.
* **Weather:** historical Open-Meteo reanalysis is implemented and exercised. Explicit EPW/TMY/CSV import is implemented but not validated against a bundled licensed EPW. Direct NSRDB acquisition and annual scenario validation remain outstanding. A TMY/historical-price pairing is a hybrid scenario.
* **Electrical physics:** balanced three-phase AC only; no phase unbalance, faults, protection, harmonic, dynamic or real Con Edison network model. Meshed networks support local simulation and snapshot AC OPF, not the radial MPC formulation. No conic branch-flow or exact nonlinear multiperiod OPF is implemented. There is no automatic corrective-dispatch policy after an AC/realised-comfort violation; the run is flagged and excluded from investment results.
* **Devices:** PV/BESS/HVAC/EV are coupled to dispatch. Hot-water storage and generic external wind curves are standalone tested functions, not dispatch-integrated resources. No manufacturer wind-curve dataset, real wind location, diesel/fuel/emissions model, islanding, device-specific calendar ageing, V2G or EnergyPlus calibration is provided. No rooftop wind or urban utility-scale solar farm is invented.
* **Controls:** sharing ADMM is genuine convex P/Q decomposition, executed sequentially in one Python process. It is validated on small cases, not guaranteed to converge within default limits for every large horizon. Mixed-integer distributed operation and negative prices are rejected; central MILP handles the latter. Heating/cooling mode is seasonally fixed in predictive control. Forecast persistence/day-lag models are implemented; regression, XGBoost, trained chronological evaluation and forecast-error Monte Carlo control experiments are not.
* **Market rules:** retail rates and export compensation are illustrative assumptions. Verified Con Edison service-class tariff reconstruction, demand-charge billing histories, NYISO eligibility/publication-time certification, service qualification and actual programme settlement are not implemented. Future realised prices are excluded from MPC. Hypothetical service revenue is reported separately from operating savings.
* **Finance:** full lifecycle arithmetic, assumed uncertainty distributions and discrete design-grid dispatch sizing are implemented. The interactive annual benefit extrapolates the selected short simulation using declared representative days. It is not an observed annual saving. Continuous investment/operation co-optimisation, taxes, subsidies, fuel and validated lifetime prediction are not implemented. No-DER investment comparison and same-DER control comparison are separately labelled.
* **Flexibility:** forecast-conditional multi-period envelopes and realised event delivery are distinct. No field certification or guaranteed envelope under arbitrary forecast errors is asserted. Activation delay, notification eligibility and resource-category settlement filtering are not fully modelled. Recovery is evaluated over the available four-hour post-event window; an event near the end of an execution cannot demonstrate complete recovery.
* **Scale:** 1,000 buildings and four coupled DER categories per building are independently represented. Actual short-horizon scaling tests are provided. A 96-step, 24-hour rolling MPC benchmark at 1,000 buildings has not been completed; performance cannot be extrapolated linearly. The 3D view limits detailed meshes for large portfolios while retaining individual numerical assets.
* **Interface and exports:** all nine workspaces use executed energy states. The detailed Three.js district uses observed footprints, NYC street centre-lines and hydrography. Window positions, façade materials, roof equipment, planting, road dressing and bridge elevations are estimates, not a photogrammetric replica. Moving traffic follows a stored illustrative Python scenario, separate from electrical EV assets. Electrical overlay animation follows calculated branch-flow magnitudes/signs on synthetic connections. CSV, Parquet, PNG and SVG exports work. Automated PDF reports, a full suite of 25 publication figures, annual/monthly visual analysis and rendering of external utility-scale infrastructure remain outstanding.
* **Deployment:** local Dash application with a single cooperative worker and SQLite persistence. No multi-user authentication, deployment hardening or utility-control integration. Cancellation waits for the current solver call. This is appropriate for a local research laboratory.

These are explicit boundaries, not completed features hidden behind inactive controls. A solver failure or physical violation is preserved; it never becomes a successful zero-cost schedule. See `docs/stages.md` for files and verification evidence by development stage.
