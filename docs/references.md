# Checked sources

Sources were checked during implementation on 2 November 2026. Links point to source institutions, authors or official software documentation. No unverified DOI is assigned to the generic first-law balances.

1. **Farivar, M. and Low, S. H.** Branch Flow Model: Relaxations and Convexification. Author manuscript [arXiv:1204.4865](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.4865), Parts I/II subsequently published in IEEE Transactions on Power Systems, 2013. This is a formulation reference; this application does not claim to implement the conic relaxation or inherit its exactness conditions.
2. **Baran, M. E. and Wu, F. F.** Optimal sizing of capacitors placed on a radial distribution system. IEEE Transactions on Power Delivery 4(1), 735–743, 1989. [US DOE OSTI record](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/6373292), DOI [10.1109/61.19266](https://doi.org/10.1109/61.19266). DistFlow's original radial balance tradition motivates the approximation here; local parameter choices are assumptions.
3. **Boyd, S., Parikh, N., Chu, E., Peleato, B. and Eckstein, J.** Distributed Optimization and Statistical Learning via the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers. Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning 3(1), 1–122, 2011. [Authors' page](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/admm_distr_stats.html); DOI [10.1561/2200000016](https://doi.org/10.1561/2200000016). Basis for two-block sharing updates and absolute/relative residual stopping criteria.
4. **Rawlings, J. B., Mayne, D. Q. and Diehl, M. M.** Model Predictive Control: Theory, Computation, and Design. [Authors' textbook site](https://sites.engineering.ucsb.edu/~jbraw/mpc/). Basis for receding-horizon state feedback, applying one action and re-solving.
5. **pvlib developers.** [PVWatts DC equation](https://pvlib-python.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/generated/pvlib.pvsystem.pvwatts_dc.html), [Faiman model](https://pvlib-python.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/generated/pvlib.temperature.faiman.html), [EPW reader](https://pvlib-python.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/generated/pvlib.iotools.read_epw.html). Runtime version is pinned to 0.15.2; inspect local documentation when changing versions. Original PVWatts/Faiman references are documented by these implementations.
6. **Sandia National Laboratories.** [PV Performance Modelling Collaborative](https://pvpmc.sandia.gov/), a primary source collection for physically meaningful photovoltaic modelling.
7. **pandapower developers.** [Nonlinear AC OPF / PYPOWER documentation, version 3.5.6](https://pandapower.readthedocs.io/en/v3.5.6/opf/pypower_run.html). Documents supported controllable quantities, constraints and convergence limitations. Used for independent AC verification and snapshot OPF.
8. **HiGHS developers.** [Official solver site](https://highs.dev/). LP, QP and mixed-integer capabilities; SOCP and nonlinear AC are not claimed HiGHS capabilities.
9. **NYC OTI.** [Building footprint metadata](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md), [dataset](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/5zhs-2jue). Geometry, BIN, BBL, roof height and record interpretation.
10. **NYC DCP.** [PLUTO](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/64uk-42ks). Observed tax-lot characteristics; not a one-record-per-building energy inventory.
11. **NYISO.** [Day-ahead zonal LBMP](https://mis.nyiso.com/public/P-2Alist.htm), [real-time integrated reports](https://mis.nyiso.com/public/P-4Alist.htm), and native historical real-time CSV archives identified in provenance. Prices are USD/MWh. Eligibility and publication-time-certified predictive scheduling are not implemented.
12. **Open-Meteo / ECMWF.** [Historical reanalysis documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api). Default weather source, hourly radiation semantics and attribution. This is reanalysis, not measured rooftop weather.
13. **Con Edison.** [Official rates and tariffs](https://www.coned.com/en/rates-tariffs/rates). Identifies authoritative service-class schedules. The illustrative tariff in this repository is not derived from or represented as an applicable quoted schedule.
14. **US DOE / NREL.** [Decarbonizing Building Thermal Systems](https://betterbuildingssolutioncenter.energy.gov/sites/default/files/attachments/87812.pdf). Physical context for heat pump COP and thermal energy service. It does not validate this project's assumed COP curve or RC parameters.

Battery, hot-water and lumped-building equations follow energy conservation; their declared parameters require empirical calibration. Discounted cash flow is explicitly implemented as a sum of discounted annual cash flows and independently checked against hand-computable cases. The project makes no claim of a specific empirical battery degradation or building model being validated merely because its governing balance is correct.
